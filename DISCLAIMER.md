# Disclaimer

Hermes Control Pack is an agent-harness research project.

Prompt, context, skill, orchestration, and verification techniques can improve workflow discipline and reliability, but they cannot reproduce proprietary model weights, private inference systems, unavailable tools, or another provider's hidden infrastructure.

Research-corpus metadata may be incomplete or incorrect. Captured prompts can be stale, modified, mislabeled, product-specific, or mutually contradictory. HCP therefore treats them as research evidence rather than authoritative runtime instructions.

Benchmark outcomes depend heavily on the base model, provider, tool configuration, repository, task difficulty, and evaluation method. Do not attribute a result to HCP without controlling those variables.
